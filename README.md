# ransomware-detection-prevention-study
Study on Detection and Prevention of Ransomware Attacks

📌 Overview

This project presents a literature-based study of techniques used to detect and prevent ransomware attacks. It explores existing cybersecurity approaches, their advantages and limitations, and challenges in protecting systems from ransomware threats.

🎯 Objectives

- Study existing ransomware detection and prevention techniques.
- Review research papers and understand current approaches.
- Compare security techniques based on their effectiveness and limitations.
- Identify research gaps and potential areas for future investigation.

🔍 Research Areas

- Ransomware detection techniques
- Behaviour-based and signature-based detection
- Machine learning approaches for ransomware detection
- Ransomware prevention and mitigation
- Challenges and future research directions

🛠️ Methodology

- Review relevant research papers and literature.
- Study existing ransomware detection and prevention approaches.
- Compare their working principles, strengths, and limitations.
- Identify research challenges and possible future improvements.

📚 Key Learning Outcomes

- Improved understanding of ransomware threats and cybersecurity.
- Knowledge of existing detection and prevention techniques.
- Experience in reviewing technical literature and identifying research gaps.

🚀 Future Scope

Future work may explore machine learning-based ransomware detection, intelligent threat analysis, and improved security mechanisms.
